# Firmware F1.3.7dev16 — selectable Phasor and IIgs sound clocks

One complete Phasor can occupy **slot 4 or slot 2** on all supported hosts.
Choose Off, Slot 4, or Slot 2 in the Phasor tab. The Slot 2 tab also offers
Phasor. Audio preferences follow the card; moving it resets its device and
IRQ state. Selecting another slot-2 card turns the slot-2 Phasor off.
Slot 2 and slot 4 cannot host two Phasors at once.

The IIgs sound clock now advances during unrelated bus cycles, so AY tones
and the extra native-mode clock do not depend on accesses to the card.
Slot ownership still controls register replies and IRQ output. Remapping
an enabled slot preserves the card's state and elapsed audio time.

## IIgs setup

- Keep Appletini in physical slot 3 and enable the **IIgs physical slot 3
  (after restart)** profile, then restart Appletini.
- Leave GS slot 3 on its internal 80-column setting. Set the chosen logical
  Phasor slot to **Your Card**, with no physical card conflicting in it.
- On ROM 1, choosing Phasor in slot 2 lets slot 4 remain internal for the
  native mouse. Slot 2 then replaces the internal modem service.
- Phasor uses neither `/INH` nor `/DMA`. The GS restrictions on acceleration
  and host-memory replacement remain in force.

The tester's Pure Noise Mockingboard-detection failure remains open.
Its exact timer probe passes simulation, including without the AY clock
fix. UART and board evidence are still needed to establish the cause.

## Build and validation

This package uses the exact user-selected m250 FPGA build:
`20261111T203601Z-09a3bd15-full`. Packaging does not rebuild the FPGA.

- Setup WNS **+0.106 ns**, hold **+0.033 ns**, pulse width **+0.265 ns**.
- No failing endpoints; route, bus skew, and checked constraint bounds pass.
- The original run used a +0.000 ns export gate. A separate package check
  verifies that the measured result clears +0.050 ns; the original manifest
  remains unchanged.
- The selected bitstream, XSA, checkpoint, and 680 HDL/script inputs match
  their recorded hashes. The XSA contains that exact bitstream.
- The single-card simulation passed 2,562 checks; all 11 GS safety benches
  passed. Directed tests cover each slot's IRQ permission and state across
  live GS remapping. Host tests cover shared audio settings, old FPGA
  compatibility, profile key order, and slot slowdown.
- The full config-profile suite retains its pre-existing menu-shell failure.

The package contains a fresh XSA-derived platform, FSBL, and both ARM cores.
The build record and image audit document their exact inputs and payloads.
Golden Boot remains **B2.0.6**; no Golden update is included.

This image has not been flashed or tested on a physical board. Install
`FIRMWARE.BIN` through the normal firmware updater and test Phasor detection,
audio, speech, IRQs, and each selected slot. Native GS video still needs
physical timing and throughput validation.
