Appletini ONE

Installation and quick start

FirmwareF1.1.1installed

This document tells you how to install the Appletini ONE card in your Apple //e and how to start it for the first time. Read all the steps before you start.

CAUTION
  • Turn off the Apple //e and disconnect its power cable before you install or remove the card.
  • Static electricity can cause damage to the card. Touch the metal case of the power supply before you touch the card.
  • Hold the card by its edges. Do not touch the gold contacts.

Examine the microSD card

The microSD card is already installed in the top slot of the Appletini ONE. Shipping can push the microSD card out of its slot.

  1. Find the microSD slot on the top edge of the card (item B in the photo below).
  2. Make sure that the microSD card is fully in the slot.
  3. If the microSD card is not fully in the slot, push it in until it stops.

Install the microSD card

This package does not contain a microSD card. You must supply one. The Appletini ONE keeps its settings and disk images on the microSD card.

  1. Find the microSD slot on the top edge of the card (item B in the photo below).
  2. Hold the microSD card with its contacts toward the card.
  3. Push the microSD card fully into the slot, until it stops.
NOTE

Install the microSD card before you turn on the computer. The card cannot start without it.

Install the card in slot 7

  1. Remove the lid of the Apple //e.
  2. Find slot 7. Slot 7 is the slot that is farthest from the power supply.
  3. Remove the cover from the rear-panel opening that is nearest to slot 7, if necessary.
  4. Align the edge contacts (F) with slot 7. The connectors must point to the rear of the computer.
  5. Push the card straight down until it is fully in the slot. Do not rock the card.
  6. Make sure that the card does not touch other cards.

Connect the cables

Connect all the cables before you turn on the computer.

  1. Connect the mini-HDMI cable to the video output (C). Connect the other end to your monitor or TV.
  2. Connect the 12 V power adapter to the power input (A). Connect the adapter to a mains outlet.
  3. If you have a USB mouse or the USB keypad, connect it to the lower USB-C port (D). This port is green.
Power adapter: 12 V DC, 3 AVideo: mini-HDMI, 1920 × 1080Mouse / keypad: green USB-C port
CAUTION

Use only a 12 V DC, 3 A power adapter. An incorrect adapter can cause damage to the card.

Start the computer

  1. Put the lid back on the Apple //e. Connect the power cable of the Apple //e.
  2. Turn on the monitor. Then turn on the Apple //e.
  3. When the computer starts, push A. The Appletini boot menu opens.
  4. In the boot menu, select a SmartPort slot. Then select a disk image to load in that slot.
NOTE

To go back to the A:APPLETINI prompt at any time, push Ctrl + Open-Apple + Reset together.

Use the USB keypad

Connect the keypad to the lower USB-C port (green). Use the keypad to control the Appletini menu and the speed of the Apple //e. Grey keys have no function.

Update the firmware

The card is supplied with firmware F1.1.1 installed. You do not have to update it now. When a new version is available, do these steps:

  1. Download the new FIRMWARE.BIN file from appletini.org.
  2. Turn off the Apple //e. Remove the microSD card from the card.
  3. Copy FIRMWARE.BIN to the root folder of the microSD card.
  4. Push the microSD card fully back into the top slot. Turn on the Apple //e.
  5. Wait. The card writes and checks the new firmware. Then it changes the file name to FIRMWARE.OK and starts normally.
CAUTION

Do not turn off the power during the update. If you turn off the power, the update stops and you must do it again.
